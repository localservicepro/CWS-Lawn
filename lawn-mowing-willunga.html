Signs to Watch For

Signs Your Willunga Lawn Needs Professional Attention

Not sure if it's time to call in the professionals? Here are the signs Willunga homeowners most commonly notice before booking a lawn mowing service.

01

Grass Above 5cm

Grass left over 5cm — especially Buffalo and Kikuyu — becomes increasingly difficult to mow cleanly and can harbour pests, weeds, and thatch buildup beneath the surface.

02

Uneven or Patchy Growth

Bare patches, uneven growth, or weeds pushing through are signs your lawn needs more than a mow. Our team can assess whether a treatment or renovation is also required.

03

Overgrown Edges & Borders

Edges creeping into garden beds, paths, or driveways make properties look untidy fast. Regular lawn mowing includes clean edge trimming as standard with every visit.

04

Lawn Feels Soft or Spongy

A spongy feeling underfoot often indicates thatch buildup or compaction — both common in Willunga's clay-heavy soils. A lawn mowing schedule combined with seasonal aeration addresses this effectively.

05

Yellowing After Each Mow

If your lawn goes yellow after mowing, the grass is likely being cut too short or too infrequently. A professional lawn mowing service sets the correct cut height for your grass type.

06

No Time or Capacity

Sometimes the sign is simple — you're too busy to keep up with it. A regular lawn mowing schedule from CWS takes the task off your plate entirely, every fortnight.

Frequently Asked Questions About Lawn Mowing in Willunga

Answers to the questions we hear most from homeowners and property managers across Willunga and the Fleurieu Peninsula.

What is professional lawn mowing and what does it include? +

Professional lawn mowing in Willunga covers the full service — mowing at the correct height for your grass type, edge trimming along all paths, garden beds, and driveways, and a full blow-down of hard surfaces after the job. We handle clippings and leave your property clean. CWS carries out this full-service visit for residential and commercial properties across the Fleurieu Peninsula.

How much does lawn mowing in Willunga cost? +

Costs vary depending on the size of your lawn, the current length of the grass, and whether you need acreage mowing or a standard residential cut. We provide free, transparent quotes before any work begins.

How often should I have my lawn mowed in Willunga? +

During the warmer months (Spring/Summer), we recommend fortnightly mowing to keep up with rapid growth. In Autumn and Winter, monthly visits are usually sufficient for most Fleurieu Peninsula properties.

What types of properties do you mow across the Fleurieu Peninsula? +

We service everything from compact courtyard gardens and standard residential blocks to large acreage properties, commercial sites, and strata complexes.

Why does my lawn go yellow after mowing? +

This usually happens if the grass is cut too short (scalping) or if it hasn't been mowed frequently enough, exposing the lower, un-sunned stalks. Our team ensures the correct deck height is set for your specific grass type.

Do you offer ongoing lawn mowing schedules in Willunga? +

Yes, we highly recommend our regular scheduled service. It guarantees your property is maintained consistently without you having to remember to book each time.
