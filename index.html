Our Story

A Family-Run Business Built on the Fleurieu Peninsula

CWS Lawn and Garden Care was built on a simple belief — that the properties people live in and work from deserve to be looked after properly. Not rushed, not cut short, and not handed off to someone who doesn't know the land.

Based at 14 High St in Willunga, CWS has grown into one of the Fleurieu Peninsula's most trusted outdoor services teams — covering lawn mowing, acreage mowing, gardening and pruning, lawn renovations, weed control, and block slashing across 15+ suburbs from Seaford to Encounter Bay.

We started small, doing what we know best — turning up on time, doing quality work, and leaving every property better than we found it. That approach hasn't changed. Most of our clients find us through a neighbour or a referral.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you service? +

We service 15+ suburbs across the Fleurieu Peninsula, from Seaford in the north to Encounter Bay in the south, and inland to Mount Compass, Kuitpo, and Whites Valley. Based at 14 High St, Willunga SA 5172.

How much does lawn mowing in Willunga cost? +

Costs vary depending on the size of your lawn, the current length of the grass, and whether you need acreage mowing or a standard residential cut. We provide free, transparent quotes before any work begins.

How often should I have my lawn mowed? +

During the warmer months (Spring/Summer), we recommend fortnightly mowing to keep up with rapid growth. In Autumn and Winter, monthly visits are usually sufficient for most Fleurieu Peninsula properties.

Do you handle acreage and rural blocks? +

Yes. Our acreage mowing service is designed for properties of 1 acre and above, using commercial-grade ride-on mowers and slashers. We regularly handle everything from 1-acre lifestyle blocks to 50+ acre rural landholdings.

Are you fully insured? +

Every CWS job is carried out with full public liability insurance. Your property, your fencing, and our team are covered — giving you complete confidence on every visit.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance schedules? +

Yes, we highly recommend our regular scheduled service. It guarantees your property is maintained consistently without you having to remember to book each time.
