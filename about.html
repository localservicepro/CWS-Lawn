Our Story

A Family-Run Business Built on the Fleurieu Peninsula

CWS Lawn and Garden Care was built on a simple belief — that the properties people live in and work from deserve to be looked after properly. Not rushed, not cut short, and not handed off to someone who doesn't know the land.

Based at 14 High St in Willunga, CWS has grown into one of the Fleurieu Peninsula's most trusted outdoor services teams — covering lawn mowing, acreage mowing, gardening and pruning, lawn renovations, weed control, and block slashing across 15+ suburbs from Seaford to Encounter Bay.

We started small, doing what we know best — turning up on time, doing quality work, and leaving every property better than we found it. That approach hasn't changed. Most of our clients find us through a neighbour or a referral. That's not an accident — it's the result of delivering the same professional standard on every visit, for every property, regardless of size.

Willunga's Mediterranean climate, clay-dominant soils, and the mix of residential, rural, and coastal properties across the Fleurieu Peninsula create a genuinely distinct outdoor environment. We've built our service around that specific context — not a generic national template, but practical, local knowledge that produces real results for the properties we care for.

What We Stand For

The CWS Standards That Drive Every Job on the Fleurieu Peninsula

We're not the cheapest service on the peninsula and we don't try to be. What we offer is consistent quality, reliability, and local expertise — the three things that matter most when you're trusting someone to look after your property.

01

Show Up On Time

We confirm every appointment and we arrive when we say we will. Fleurieu Peninsula homeowners and rural property owners have enough to manage without chasing a contractor who doesn't turn up. Reliability isn't an extra — it's the foundation of the service.

02

Diagnose Before Acting

We assess your property before recommending any treatment or service. The right solution for a compacted Willunga clay lawn is not the same as the right solution for a hydrophobic sandy lawn near Aldinga Beach. Honest diagnosis leads to better results and no wasted spend.

03

Quote Before Starting

You receive a clear price before any work begins. No open day rates, no post-job surprises. What we quote is what you pay. This is how we'd want to be treated as customers ourselves — and it's how we run every CWS job across the Fleurieu Peninsula.

04

Leave It Better Than We Found It

Every CWS visit ends the same way — the property is cleaner, tidier, and in better condition than when we arrived. We blow down paths, remove clippings and green waste, and make sure the result is something you're genuinely proud of when you see it.

05

Local Knowledge, Applied

Years of working across Willunga, McLaren Vale, Aldinga, Mount Compass, and the wider Fleurieu Peninsula have given us an understanding of local soils, grass varieties, seasonal conditions, and fire management requirements that directly improves the quality of our work.

06

Honest Recommendations

If your lawn doesn't need a renovation program — we'll tell you. If block slashing alone won't solve your weed problem — we'll explain why. CWS gives you an honest assessment and the right recommendation every time, even when it means a smaller job than you'd anticipated.
